Belt Sort: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Belt Sort mobile game ("the app"), published by Qubic Digital Services, handles.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, best moves), settings, and an in-progress level snapshot are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, require an account, or collect this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions. How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, or a region with similar rules, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new date.
